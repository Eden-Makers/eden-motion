## Typography
- Family: Neue Haas Grotesk Display / Helvetica Now Display, Medium (500) for headlines and words, Medium/Bold for numerals. Free alternative: **Inter Tight** (Medium 500, numbers 500-600) or **Inter Display**.
- Tracking: tight, about -2% to -3% (letter-spacing -0.02em to -0.03em). Leading on the word stack ~0.82-0.85 (lines nearly touch).
- Sizes (font size relative to frame height H): counter numerals ~0.36H (cap height ~0.27H, cropped by frame), labels "Trades/Value" ~0.055H; headline "The Frontier..." ~0.10H with 3 staggered lines; word stack ~0.12H; currency pair ~0.13H; tier list numbers ~0.055H; wordmark final ~0.06H.
- Animation styles:
  - Counter with ghost trails: main digits crisp, 4-6 copies offset +x with decreasing opacity and horizontal blur (directional echo). Digits roll continuously.
  - Line-by-line pop for headline (each line appears on a frame step, no fade), then 3D rods drop on top.
  - Vertical word-stack scroll, linear, refracted by glass.
  - Label swap behind glass (hard swaps every 6-12 frames).
  - Wordmark: horizontal slide with motion blur, expo-out.
- Verbatim copy: "Trades", "Value", "The Frontier", "is the edge", "of opportunity", "Trades/Balance/Profit" on cube faces, trading words listed in shot 7, "EUR/USD", "NZD/USD", "AUD/USD" (03 also shows GBP/JPY, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, EUR/JPY, USD/SEK, USD/MXN), "Tier 1/2/3", "140,000 Pts", "112,000 Pts", "96,000 Pts", "4.2%", "3.6%", "FrontierTrader", "79,367", "First Trade", "Nov 2023", "Most Traded", "RAY", "Favorite Trade", "Raydium", "Frontier Traders".

## 3D / materials / lighting
- Chrome hardware: polished/brushed stainless (Principled metallic 1.0, roughness 0.12-0.2, anisotropic subtle on rods). Lit by a large soft studio HDRI or 2-3 big area softboxes; reflections show white strips and grey floor gradient. No visible floor, no shadows, no DOF, light motion blur on spins (shutter ~0.5).
- Glass letters: extruded grotesk, frosted glass (transmission 1, roughness ~0.35) with grunge/smudge roughness map, bright rim/fresnel edges; black world, a single strong backlight producing bloom where the logo sits. Text plates behind are refracted and blurred.
- Data cube: black cube, faces with emissive white text textures, mild bloom.
- B&W footage: crushed blacks, blown whites, heavy motion blur; reads as silhouette.
- No grain, no chromatic aberration. Clean editorial finish.

## Signature techniques
- 24-frame hard-cut metronome; whoosh accent ~7 frames before each cut.
- Polarity flipping: white / black / white frames alternate almost every shot.
- Counter with directional ghost echo (copies at x offsets ~0.04W, 0.08W, 0.12W..., opacity 0.6/0.4/0.25/0.15, blur 8-20 px).
- 3D objects dropped onto 2D typography (rods across headline).
- Flow field of ~100 dashes (length ~0.05H, stroke ~0.006H) rotating to a moving attractor.
- Glass-letter foreground layer over swapping labels (logo, currency pairs, word stack).
- Logo built from the same "dash" vocabulary (5 bars), then wordmark slides in.
