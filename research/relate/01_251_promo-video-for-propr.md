## Typography
- Family: grotesk with straight-tail "y", double-story "a", open apertures; matches **Space Grotesk** closely (or a commercial like Aeonik / Neue Machina light). Free alternative: **Space Grotesk** 400 for captions, 600-700 for numbers.
- Weights: captions Regular 400, big number Bold 700, lockup wordmarks Medium in caps ("MMT", "PROPR", tracking +4%).
- Sizes relative to H: caption ~0.045H (inside a card with ~0.02H padding, hairline 1 px border white @ 20%, fill black @ 35-50%); "$300,000" ~0.25H; "Keep 80% of the profit" ~0.075H; subline ~0.04H; lockup ~0.05H.
- Tracking: near 0 for captions, slightly negative (-1%) for big number.
- Animation: (a) boxed-glyph constellation fly-in with dotted connector lines, (b) typewriter with a white block cursor, (c) scattered-words-in-depth converge, (d) blur-in for big numbers with digital stutter, (e) green highlight on key word (80%, "fun...").
- Verbatim copy: "You now read the market better than ever", "So we brought in more size", "Propr funded trading accounts, now native in MMT", "Prove your skill", "$300,000", "Get funded up to", "Keep 80% of the profit", "Paid out instantly in USDC onchain", "Additional size, in the terminal you trust", "MMT", "x", "PROPR".

## 3D / materials / lighting
- Candlesticks: emerald glass/acrylic (transmission, slight emission inside), bloom, volumetric god rays from top, aurora-like green light sheets (likely volumetric planes or 2D light textures).
- Jet footage: AI-generated (Kling/Veo style artifacts: morphing wings, inconsistent jet geometry between shots), teal-orange grade, camera shake.
- Global finish: heavy bloom on whites, vignette, rainbow light-leak wipes, slight chromatic aberration on glitch frames, film grain light.

## Signature techniques
- HUD tracking language: thin boxes + dotted lines that lock onto subjects (engines, jet) and onto letters.
- Caption card as the constant typographic container across cuts (subtitle-like but designed).
- Rainbow/thermal light-leak gradient wipes (4.5 s, 13.0 s).
- Key-word color highlight in brand green.
