## Typography
- Minimal on-screen type, all inside the 3D world: UI labels in a neutral grotesk (closest **Inter** 500/600), notification pills ~0.03 of cell height, ticker list names ~0.06h. Wordmark "arcus" lowercase rounded geometric (closest free: **Quicksand** / **Nunito** 600, or Manrope), debossed.
- Animation: pills pop with scale overshoot (back-out), cards scroll, logo pieces assemble. No overlay titles.

## 3D / materials / lighting
- Materials: matte clay/plastic (roughness 0.5-0.7, subtle subsurface on beige), satin sage plastic with soft bevels, frosted glass pills (transmission, roughness 0.3) with white text, light wood, slight metallic on coins (rose gold, silver) with low gloss.
- Lighting: warm low sun key (~4500 K) through window, long soft shadows, strong bounce, warm grey ambient; night variants with only screen emission.
- Camera: long lens (85-135 mm), shallow DOF (f/2-2.8 look), very slow moves. Mild bloom on screens, no visible grain, no CA. Look is Octane/Redshift "soft product world".

## Signature techniques
- 2x2 grid of separate scenes cut on independent beats.
- Lights-off/lights-on cut inside a single shot as a rhythmic beat (TL, TR).
- News headlines as floating frosted-glass pills around a clay globe.
- UI rendered as physical objects (curled paper card, stacked extruded list cards).
