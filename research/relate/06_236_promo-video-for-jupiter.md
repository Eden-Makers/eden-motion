## Typography
- Main family: neo-grotesk, Medium (Jupiter brand). Free alternative: **Inter** 500 / **Geist** 500, tracking -1%.
- Display: custom chunky toy/3D lettering (bubble letters with flowers). Free alternative for a base: **Fredoka** 700 or **Baloo 2** 800, extruded in Blender.
- Sizes: headlines ~0.05H (small, centered), giant background words ~0.30H ("One Piece", "EPIC").
- Animation: per-word blur-in, split-around-object layout, giant words sliding behind a character, motion-blur slide on end title.
- Verbatim copy: "The place to collect", "Your", "Memories", "Jupiter Gacha", "Is now native on Jupiter Mobile", "Rip packs anywhere", "One Piece", "Pokemon", "Spin and tap to open a pack", "Trace line to open", "2000", "Year", "PSA MINT 9", "Grade", "EPIC", "Rarity", "Pull it", "Keep the slab", "Or take the instant buyback", "Buy again", "Buyback 230,000", "Catch 'em all", "on Jupiter Mobile", "Available on", "App Store", "Google Play".

## 3D / materials / lighting
Booster packs: foil wrappers with holographic prints, glossy specular; pokeball: glossy plastic; toy letters: candy plastic with emissive accents and bloom. Global: barrel lens distortion + vignette + chromatic aberration in night section, bloom, light rays.

## Signature techniques
- Painted anime sky as a persistent world binding UI and 3D.
- Plain word -> toy 3D word rebuild on the beat.
- Product object spinning between two halves of a split headline.
