## Typography
- Sans: light neo-grotesk with very tight tracking (glyphs almost touching), like **Neue Montreal Light / Inter Display Light**. Free alternative: **Inter Tight 300** at letter-spacing -0.04em.
- Serif italic for emphasis words and logo: classic high-contrast italic (Times / PP Editorial New Italic feel). Free alternative: **Instrument Serif Italic** (headline emphasis) and **Newsreader Display Italic 600** for the bolder "Flex" logo.
- Sizes relative to H: headlines ~0.085H; city captions ~0.09H; "170+ Countries" ~0.12H; end logo ~0.11H; tagline "Go Global with Flex" ~0.03H.
- Animation: per-word blur-in (blur 12 px -> 0, opacity 0 -> 1, 0.4 s, stagger 0.15 s); letter cascade dropping from above (Apple Pay); label scramble on the button; crossfade swaps on cities; mixed sans + serif italic in one line.
- Verbatim copy: "Flex", "Modern banking meets stablecoins", "USDT", "USD", "USDC", "Create card", "VISA", "Infinite Business", "Move money at internet speed", "Credit", "US banking", "Global banking", "Stablecoins", "Learn more", "From", "USDC", "$341,999.21 available", "To", "AP/OPs", "Checking ..0098", "Money from San Francisco", "to New York in seconds", "to Monaco in seconds", "to Kuala Lumpur in seconds", "to London in seconds", "to Seoul in seconds", "170+ Countries", "Spend your stablecoins on Apple Pay", "Hold Near Reader", "Done", "Without Borders", "Without Banking Delays", "Built for Speed", "Built for Ambition", "Built for Owners", "Built for You", "Go Global with Flex".

## 3D / materials / lighting
- Cards: brushed aluminium/steel in green anodized and raw silver, deep bevel, cutout "F" relief, studio softbox reflections, strong motion blur on fan rotation, no DOF.
- UI product illustrations: small 3D props on marble plinths (black card, Greek temple, brass globe, gold coin stack on green marble) - photoreal product renders.
- Globe: particle streaks / line curves, emissive green, no bloom.
- Transitions: chromatic fringe on the cloud wipe, soft blur.

## Signature techniques
- Sans + serif-italic emphasis pairing ("Built for *Speed*").
- Line-art circles that track words and morph into a venn diagram.
- Rapid 4-5 frame city montage with the sentence continuing over cuts.
- Whole-frame-to-card morph: a full-bleed section shrinks into a UI element.
