## 3D / materials / lighting
- Whole piece is one consistent "clay world": matte, slightly subsurface plastics; nothing glossy except phone glass and coin rims.
- Office: soft area light from window + warm bounce, filmic tone mapping, low contrast; night version = blue moon fill + monitor emissive.
- Day desk: hard sun with gobo (window mullion) shadows, warm 4500K; animated sun direction for time-lapse.
- Globe: displacement continents, single soft key from upper-left, no specular.
- Green blocks: rubbery satin (roughness ~0.5), rounded bevels (bevel 0.1 x size), sheen.
- Pills: frosted glass (transmission + roughness 0.35) with UI text texture.
- Strong DOF in macros (f/1.4 feel), mild bloom on emissive screens, real motion blur on whips and fast objects, very light grain.

## Signature techniques
- Narrative loop: office at 4:00 -> news -> product -> office at 4:01 with product in the world. Bookends via the flip clock.
- Lights-off / day-night flicker as transitions instead of cuts (3.9-4.5 s, 18.0-19.0 s).
- UI turned into physical objects: notification pills as frosted 3D slabs, paper UI that curls, extruded ticker rows, 3D cursor arrow.
- Monochrome clay palette with a single green brand accent.
- Long holds (2-5 s per shot), slow ease-in-out camera; only one whip pan.
- End card: logo over darkened 3D scene, not over flat black.
